3. CURRENT ACCOUNT/CARE-SPACE ARCHITECTURE DISCOVERED
auth.users is the one stable identity; care_spaces.bootstrap_owner_id is
explicitly commented in its own migration as "idempotency metadata only;
access is granted exclusively through membership" (nullable since Phase
18B) - NOT an authority field and must not be repurposed for billing;
care_space_memberships.role (organiser/contributor/viewer) is the sole
security/collaboration concept, and a care space can already have more
than one organiser. No commercial/billing concept exists anywhere today.
Full detail: proposal document section 2.

4. RECOMMENDED COMMERCIAL OWNERSHIP MODEL
A new, dedicated care_spaces.commercial_owner_id (references auth.users),
set once at creation, structurally independent of bootstrap_owner_id and
of role=organiser. Two rejected alternatives (reusing bootstrap_owner_id;
deriving ownership implicitly from "the first organiser") are documented
with reasons. Full detail: section 3.

5. MULTIPLE-SUPPORTED-PERSON BEHAVIOUR
One entitled account's commercial_owner_id can cover any number of care
spaces (Beauty + Jackie) with zero extra schema or purchase - the
proposition is per-account, not per-care-space. Full detail: section 4.

6. CARE CIRCLE COLLABORATOR BEHAVIOUR
A collaborator's mutation ability depends on two independent conditions
(their own Care Circle domain grant AND the care space's own entitlement
via its commercial_owner_id) - a collaborator never needs a personal
subscription to help in someone else's entitled space. Full detail:
section 5.

7. MULTI-ORGANISER BEHAVIOUR
role=organiser is never automatically "must pay". Recommended Policy 1:
entitlement is tied to the SPECIFIC commercial_owner_id, not "any active
organiser's entitlement covers everyone" (Policy 2, explicitly rejected
for reintroducing exactly the ambiguity the brief warns against). Flagged
as open decision 1 for product-owner confirmation. Full detail: section
6.

8. BILLING-OWNER CONTINUITY MODEL
A new, narrowly-scoped transfer_care_space_commercial_ownership() RPC -
callable by the current owner (self-transfer) or, once their membership
is no longer active, by any remaining active organiser. Explicitly NOT a
general arbitrary-user-to-user transfer feature. Full detail: section 7.

9. TRIAL-START RECOMMENDATION
The first successful bootstrap_supported_people() call for an account -
not account creation/verification/login, since those precede any
Lilica-specific action. Server-authoritative, written exactly once. Full
detail: section 8.

10. EXACT ONE-MONTH SEMANTICS
Recommended: a fixed 30x24 hours from trial_started_at, not "one calendar
month" (which is genuinely ambiguous at month-end, per the brief's own
31-January example). Full detail: section 9.

11. APP-MANAGED VS STORE-MANAGED TRIAL RECOMMENDATION
Recommended: Option A, an app-managed free month with no store
subscription started until an explicit purchase decision - compared
directly against Option B (store-managed intro trial) across friction,
automatic-charging risk, fidelity to the product owner's own stated
proposition, platform-rule compatibility, conversion quality, and
entitlement-architecture simplicity. Full comparison table: section 10.

12. DIRECT STORES VS SUBSCRIPTION-MANAGEMENT SERVICE COMPARISON
Recommended: RevenueCat, specifically because Lilica's one-product/
two-platform shape is the case such a service earns its cost back
immediately (free until $2,500/month tracked revenue, verified via
current 2026 pricing research, not memory) - not "because it's popular".
Full comparison table: section 11.

13. RECOMMENDED BILLING INTEGRATION
RevenueCat wrapping native StoreKit 2 / Google Play Billing purchase
flows, with RevenueCat performing store-side verification and relaying a
single unified webhook to one Lilica Supabase Edge Function. Full detail:
sections 11, 24-25.

14. PROPOSED ENTITLEMENT STATE MACHINE
TRIAL_ACTIVE, TRIAL_EXPIRED, SUBSCRIPTION_ACTIVE, GRACE_PERIOD,
BILLING_RETRY/ACCOUNT_HOLD, SUBSCRIPTION_EXPIRED, REVOKED/REFUNDED,
UNKNOWN/VERIFICATION_REQUIRED - no ambiguous boolean flag anywhere. Full
table (mutations/read/export/Care-Circle-participation/purchase-restore/
offline per state): section 12.

15. READ-ONLY RULES
Confirmed verbatim from the brief: expiry never removes access to
previously entered information; only active care-management mutation is
gated. Full detail: section 13.

16. COMPLETE MUTATION INVENTORY
Every current server mutation path classified ENTITLEMENT REQUIRED / NOT
REQUIRED - apply_record_mutation() and its dependents, attachment/link
RPCs, invite_member()/revoke_invitation()/change_member_role()/
remove_member() all REQUIRED; accept_invitation()/decline_invitation()/
leave_care_space()/delete_my_account()/export_my_data()/
account_deletion_precheck()/every list_* read all NOT REQUIRED. First
bootstrap for a brand-new account is never gated (it is the event that
starts the trial); a second-or-later bootstrap is. Full table: section
14.

17. SERVER ENFORCEMENT DESIGN
One shared care_space_has_active_entitlement() function, called
consistently from every REQUIRED RPC, mirroring the existing
can_access_care_space_records() pattern - never duplicated bespoke logic
per RPC. Full detail: section 15.

18. OFFLINE ENTITLEMENT DESIGN
Client caches last server-verified state + last_verified_at, usable for a
proposed 72-hour offline grace window before falling back to a
"reconnect to confirm" state - never trusted as authoritative for a
server mutation regardless of the window. Full detail: section 16.

19. OFFLINE MUTATION-EXPIRY BEHAVIOUR
An offline-created mutation that reaches the server after entitlement has
genuinely expired is rejected by the server (authoritative), but the
user's work is moved to a visible "couldn't be saved - subscription
needed" holding area (reusing the existing document-cleanup-queue's own
established failed-state pattern) rather than silently discarded. Full
detail: section 16.

20. iOS -> ANDROID ENTITLEMENT BEHAVIOUR
Recommended: entitlement follows the Lilica account, not the platform/
device, once RevenueCat has verified the purchase - matching the actual
"Lilica account subscription" proposition. Full detail: section 17.

21. ANDROID -> iOS ENTITLEMENT BEHAVIOUR
Symmetric to the above. Full detail: section 17.

22. RESTORE BEHAVIOUR
Restore is essentially "log in, re-fetch the server's own entitlement
row" for reinstall/new-device/logout-login/cleared-storage cases, since
the server row is keyed to the account, never local storage. Full detail:
section 18.

23. REFUND/REVOCATION BEHAVIOUR
Reflected via the same webhook path as any other state change into
REVOKED/REFUNDED; existing data remains fully readable under the safety
principle. Full detail: section 19.

24. ACCOUNT DELETION BEHAVIOUR
delete_my_account() (Phase 18B) is never gated by entitlement. Lilica
cannot cancel an Apple/Google subscription on the user's behalf (neither
store provides that API) - deletion must clearly disclose this before
confirmation. commercial_owner_id becomes nullable and is set to null on
deletion, mirroring bootstrap_owner_id's own established pattern exactly.
Full detail: section 20.

25. SUBSCRIPTION/CARE-SPACE OWNERSHIP SEPARATION
Fully separated: commercial_owner_id (commercial, per care space),
care_space_memberships.role (security, per membership), and entitlements
(commercial, per user account) are three structurally independent
concepts, none mixed into records. Full detail: section 21.

26. PROPOSED SCHEMA
care_spaces.commercial_owner_id (new nullable column); public.entitlements
(one row per account: status, trial_started_at, trial_expires_at,
provider, provider_app_user_id, entitlement_expires_at, last_verified_at);
public.entitlement_events (append-only audit log, mirroring
care_space_activity's own immutable-log pattern); plus
care_space_has_active_entitlement() and
transfer_care_space_commercial_ownership(). For every item: purpose,
source of truth, read/write access, RLS, and retention/deletion stated
explicitly. Full detail: section 22.

27. PROPOSED RPC/SERVER CHANGES
New: care_space_has_active_entitlement(),
transfer_care_space_commercial_ownership(), an entitlement webhook Edge
Function, apply_entitlement_update(). Redefined (same signature, one
added check each): apply_record_mutation() and the other REQUIRED RPCs
from item 16. Explicitly NOT redefined: every NOT REQUIRED RPC. Full
detail: section 23.

28. STORE-VERIFICATION ARCHITECTURE
RevenueCat's SDK on-device drives the native purchase sheet; RevenueCat
performs authoritative server-to-server verification against Apple/
Google; Lilica's own server never talks to Apple/Google directly, only
verifies RevenueCat's own unified webhook signature. A client purchase
callback alone is never trusted. Full detail: section 24.

29. SERVER-NOTIFICATION/WEBHOOK ARCHITECTURE
One Edge Function receiving RevenueCat's unified webhook (itself
aggregating Apple App Store Server Notifications V2 and Google Real-time
Developer Notifications); idempotency via a provider_event_id ledger,
mirroring record_mutation_receipts' own established operation_id pattern.
Full detail: section 25.

30. SECURITY THREAT MODEL
A mitigation stated for every threat named in the brief (modified-client
premium claims, replayed receipts, expired cached entitlement, another
user's token, revoked subscription, cross-space leakage, collaborator
mutating an expired space, client clock manipulation, offline replay,
webhook spoofing). Full table: section 26.

31. PRIVACY/DATA IMPLICATIONS
No raw payment card/bank data is ever processed or stored by Lilica or
RevenueCat - only a stable non-financial App User ID and
provider-transaction identifiers. Retention/deletion follows the Phase
18B philosophy already established for every other personal data
category. Full detail: section 27.

32. APPLE COMPLIANCE FINDINGS
Sourced from Apple's own current App Store Review Guidelines and
Auto-Renewable Subscriptions documentation (not memory): a 7-day minimum
subscription period and cross-device availability; an April 2026
guideline update requiring clear pre-purchase disclosure of what/cost/
duration/cancellation; an expected Restore Purchases affordance. Full
detail and source URLs: section 29.

33. GOOGLE PLAY COMPLIANCE FINDINGS
Sourced from Google's own current Play Billing developer documentation

launch dependency this research surfaced. Full detail and source URLs:
section 29.

34. TERMS/PRIVACY LAUNCH DEPENDENCIES

35. GBP9.99/YEAR COST SANITY CHECK
Apple/Google commission (~30% year one, ~15% thereafter under both
platforms' established subscription-commission structure) dominates;
RevenueCat's cost is negligible (free until $2,500/month tracked
revenue, then 1%); backend cost is immaterial next to Lilica's existing
Supabase footprint; VAT/sales tax is already handled by the stores acting
as merchant of record. Conclusion: the proposed architecture does not
consume an unreasonable share of the £9.99 price. Full detail: section
31.
