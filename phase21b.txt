2. PHASE 21A OPEN DECISIONS - NOW RESOLVED
============================================================

Use the following decisions as authoritative.


DECISION 1 - MULTIPLE ORGANISERS
--------------------------------

Commercial entitlement is tied to the specific:

commercial_owner_id

for the care space.

Do NOT use:

"any subscribed organiser automatically makes the care space active."

If David is commercial owner and his subscription expires while Marion is
also an organiser with her own active Lilica subscription:

the space remains read-only UNTIL commercial ownership is explicitly
transferred to Marion.

Provide a safe explicit ownership-transfer path.

No silent ownership changes.


DECISION 2 - PROFILE/ACCOUNT IDENTITY EDITS WHILE EXPIRED
---------------------------------------------------------

Account/profile identity management remains available while commercially
expired.

A user may still:

- edit their own display name;
- manage their profile photo;
- access Settings;
- manage subscription;
- restore purchases;
- export;
- leave care spaces;
- delete their account.

Commercial expiry gates CARE MANAGEMENT, not basic account control.


DECISION 3 - OFFLINE GRACE
--------------------------

Use:

72 HOURS

from the last successfully server-verified entitled state.

Within that period, the client may continue behaving as actively entitled
offline.

After 72 hours without verification:

active mutation UI should enter:

"Reconnect to confirm your subscription"

rather than assuming continued entitlement.

The server remains authoritative at all times.

Any queued mutation reaching the server after entitlement expiry must be
rejected safely and retained visibly for recovery after subscription is
restored.

Never silently discard user work.


DECISION 4 - ENTITLEMENT DATA AFTER ACCOUNT DELETION
----------------------------------------------------

When a Lilica account is deleted:

- remove/detach the user's active application entitlement linkage;
- do not keep unnecessary personal billing metadata in normal application
  tables;
- retain only the minimum provider transaction/event evidence required for
  billing integrity, reconciliation, fraud prevention, legal/accounting or
  provider obligations;
- do not store card/bank details;
- do not make retained billing audit data available to ordinary app users.

Document any provider-required retention separately.

Do not claim legal retention periods unless supported by policy/specialist
review.


DECISION 5 - REVENUECAT
-----------------------

APPROVED.

Use RevenueCat as the cross-platform subscription-management layer.

RevenueCat is approved because the product has:

- one simple annual product;
- iOS + Android;
- account-based entitlement;
- cross-platform restore;
- renewal/revocation lifecycle;
- server webhook needs.

Do not implement direct Apple/Google verification in parallel unless genuinely
required.

RevenueCat remains an implementation dependency, not the authority for Lilica
Care Circle security.


DECISION 6 - EXISTING/PRE-LAUNCH ACCOUNTS
-----------------------------------------

Lilica has not launched publicly.

Therefore:

DO NOT invent production grandfathering logic for users who do not yet exist.

For development/test accounts:

use explicit non-production test fixtures and RevenueCat sandbox state.

Do not silently derive trial history from historical account creation dates.

For future production:

new accounts begin their free period at the approved server-authoritative
trial-start event.


DECISION 7 - DEVELOPMENT / TEST ENTITLEMENTS

Use:

Any developer-only fixture mechanism must be impossible to invoke in
