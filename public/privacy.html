Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 September 2026

Lilica is a personal and family care organiser made by Luxford Interactive ("Lilica", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information Lilica collects, why, and the measures used to protect it. Lilica is not a clinical system, emergency monitoring service, or medical device, and this policy should be read with that in mind.

Information you provide

When you create a Lilica account and use the app, you may provide:

  • Your email address and a password, to create and access your account.
  • Your own name/profile details as the organiser.
  • Information about the person or people you support: their name, and any appointments, tasks, bills, home/car matters, contacts, documents, care notes, diagnosed conditions, prescribed medicines, and wellbeing updates you choose to record.
  • Photos or files you attach to a record (for example, a scanned document or a photo of a letter).

Lilica records the information you and authorised Care Circle members provide. It does not verify diagnoses or prescriptions, give medical advice, or independently draw medical, legal, emergency or monitoring conclusions.

Information collected automatically

Lilica collects a small amount of technical information needed to run the service reliably: device/app version information (for crash and compatibility diagnostics), and basic sign-in/session activity. Lilica does not use third-party advertising trackers, and does not sell your information to anyone.

How your information is used

  • To provide the core Lilica service: storing and syncing your records, scheduled items and Care Circle sharing.
  • To process your subscription (see "Payments and subscriptions" below).
  • To send account-related emails (for example, a sign-up verification code or a password reset code).
  • To respond to support requests you send us.
  • To keep the service secure and investigate misuse.

Sharing with your Care Circle

Lilica lets you invite other people ("Care Circle" members) to help with a person's care. If you invite someone, their access is limited to the categories you grant them (for example, "Care & health" or "Bills & money"). You can manage who is invited, their role and category access, and remove their access. A Care Circle member's own access is enforced by Lilica's servers, not just the app.

Where your information is stored

Lilica's data is stored using Supabase (a hosted PostgreSQL database and file storage provider). Your account information, records and any attached files are held on Supabase's infrastructure. Lilica uses encryption in transit and permission-based access controls designed to limit access to your account and the Care Circle members you authorise.

Payments and subscriptions

Lilica offers a paid annual subscription after an initial free period. Subscription purchases and card details are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play and are not provided to or stored by Lilica. Lilica uses RevenueCat to manage subscription status across devices; RevenueCat receives your subscription/purchase status from Apple or Google, not your payment details.

Email delivery

Account emails (such as sign-up verification codes and password reset codes) are sent using Resend, an email delivery provider. Resend processes your email address to deliver these messages.

Biometric app lock

If you choose to turn on Face ID/Touch ID app lock in Lilica, this uses your device's own built-in biometric system. Your device's operating system performs the biometric check; Lilica receives an authentication result rather than the biometric scan or template.

Your choices and rights

  • Export your data: Lilica lets you export a copy of your data from within the app.
  • Delete your account: Lilica lets you request account deletion from within the app. Where Lilica shares a care space with other Care Circle members, deletion may preserve minimal historical attribution (for example, "a member" performed an action) while removing your live account identity, so that other people's own records remain intact.
  • Access, correct or ask questions: contact us (see "Contact us" below) about any information Lilica holds about you.

If you are in the UK or EU, you have rights under data protection law (including the UK GDPR/EU GDPR) to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk).

Children

Lilica accounts are intended for adult organisers. Information about a person being supported (which may include a child) is entered by the organiser who creates and controls that record, not by the child themselves.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the current published version.

Contact us
