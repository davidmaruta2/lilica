Please note: These are general organisation aids, not medical, legal or financial advice. They do not replace care plans, medicines lists, professional assessments or instructions. Lilica is not an emergency service. For a life-threatening emergency call 999. If someone needs medical help now and it is not life-threatening, use the appropriate urgent-care service for your UK nation; in England, Scotland and Wales this may include NHS 111. Northern Ireland has different urgent-care arrangements.
