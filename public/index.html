Why we built it

Looking after someone is usually not just one person's job. It is often a few people, each doing what they can, and finding out about the rest later. Appointments, bills and practical follow-ups can be hard to keep track of across separate messages. We built Lilica to give invited helpers one place to organise the information they have agreed to share.

Who it's for

Lilica is for anyone helping to look after another person, such as a parent, a partner, or an older relative who needs some support with everyday life. You might be the person mainly organising things, or one of a few people helping out. Lilica works well whether it is just you, or a whole family.

Why not just use a family group chat or text messages?

Many families try this first. It usually becomes difficult to keep track of, for a few reasons.

Messages get buried
In a group chat, a question like "has anyone booked the dentist?" can get buried under other messages within a day. In Lilica, you can see straight away whether something is overdue, due today, or already done, without scrolling back through old messages to find out.
Open tasks stay visible
Recurring practical tasks can be recorded and marked complete by the people helping. Items that have not been marked complete remain visible when someone opens Lilica; the app does not monitor care or guarantee that a task will be done.
You can choose what each person sees
In a group chat, everyone usually sees everything. Lilica provides permission controls so organisers can choose which information each invited person can access. Review access and Lilica's privacy information before adding sensitive details.
You can get a clear summary when you need one
If someone new starts helping, or you are at an appointment, Lilica can put together a summary of what is happening right now. This is easier than trying to explain months of messages.

How it works

Start with the information that is useful to you and the people helping.

1
Set up who you're caring for
This could be a parent, a partner, or yourself. Add only the information that is useful to you.
2
Add what needs tracking
Appointments, bills, medicines and care needs, added as they come up.
3
Invite who's helping
Family or carers join your Care Circle with just the access you choose.
4
See shared plans in one place
Home shows recorded items that are overdue, due today and coming up to people with access who open the app.

Help them live well, and age independently.

Invited helpers can view the information shared with them when they open Lilica. You can start on your own and invite more people later.

How much does it cost?

60 days of free access. Then £8.99 a year.

The 60-day free-access period is provided and controlled by Lilica's service; it is not an App Store or Google Play introductory trial. One subscription covers the Care Circle, with no per-person charge. Check the current store listing before subscribing.
